## Universal Rules Across All Bands

1. **Prediction accuracy is never graded.** Students are scored on recording, reasoning, and completeness.
2. **Scope is content.** Over-application is a scored error from Band C upward.
3. **Affect stays low-stakes.** TFL is not a clinical model and no lesson or assessment may present it as one.
4. **Every session ends by propagating forward.** The instructional structure mirrors the framework.
